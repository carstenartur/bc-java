# ML-KEM Montgomery row products

The NTT-side dot product receives matrix or 12-bit decoded coefficients in [0,4095] and a second operand bounded by the NTT's final Barrett reduction in [0,3329]. Each of the K≤4 independent vector rows contributes a degree-one product. Summing the two diagonal products and the cross terms before the Montgomery step gives integer bounds of 54,529,020 on each diagonal and 109,058,040 on the cross term. The reduced high diagonal is multiplied by its original signed twiddle. The final outputs still pass Barrett. Intermediate representatives can change by a multiple of 3329. The independent caller-owned outputs are normalized before serialization, compression or message extraction.

The fork's keygen, encryption and decryption call paths and operand bounds were checked on ab16374d. Its native MLKEMTest and new package tests passed all 17 methods, including ACVP vectors, malformed coefficients, input preservation and exact canonical outputs. In the paired fork measurements, encapsulation/decapsulation used 1,584–3,168 fewer bytes per operation and their median elapsed time fell 4.4–10.2%. Keygen timing varied with parameter set. Full-operation speedups can vary by JVM and workload.
